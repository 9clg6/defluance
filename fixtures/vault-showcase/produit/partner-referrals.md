# Partner referrals

Raw idea from the growth offsite: accountants recommend invoicing tools to their clients, so give them a referral link and a revenue share on the first year.

Nobody owns this yet. Before it becomes a spec we need to know what share partners expect, how attribution survives a customer switching workspaces, and whether finance wants to book the share as a cost of sale.
